## 1. Project Overview

The **Mymensingh City Citizen Service Platform** (*Amar Mayor / আমার মেয়র*) is a comprehensive civic engagement, municipal governance, and public service delivery system designed specifically for the **Mymensingh City Corporation (MCC)**.

The platform bridges citizens and city administration to provide transparent complaint tracking, civic utility management, ward/zone oversight, and public accountability dashboards.

---

## 2. Scope & Target Ecosystem

* **Web Application:** Public portal, citizen self-service dashboard, MCC administrative console, Councillor & Ward Officer command center, and Mayor/Administrator monitoring panel.
* **Mobile Applications (Android & iOS):** Citizen issue reporting (with geo-tagging and multimedia proof), status tracking, emergency civic notices, and field officer task dispatch.
* **Language Support:**
  * **Bangla (বাংলা):** Primary language across citizen interfaces, administrative workflows, SMS/notifications, and reports.
  * **English:** Secondary language for internationalization, technical administration, and reporting.

---

## 3. Core Modules (To Be Detailed)

1. **Citizen Issue & Complaint Lifecycle:** Geo-tagged reporting, SLA tracking, automated routing, resolution verification.
2. **MCC Administrative Hierarchy:** Mayor / Administrator oversight, Chief Executive Officer (CEO), Department heads.
3. **Zonal & Ward Administration:** 33+ Wards, Zonal Offices, Ward Councillors, and designated Ward Inspectors/Officers.
4. **Workforce & Field Operations:** Assignment of sanitation, engineering, lighting, and health workers with field audit logs.
5. **Public Accountability & Transparency Dashboard:** Live statistics on complaint resolution rates, ward performance leaderboards, and public notices.
6. **Notification & Alert Hub:** Bangla SMS, push notifications, and automated escalation triggers.
